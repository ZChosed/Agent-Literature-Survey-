# Screening criteria

Write the inclusion and exclusion criteria the model and the human rater both apply.
Give each criterion an ID at the start of a line followed by a dash; the IDs are
parsed and enforced (a response citing an unknown ID fails validation).

## Decision procedure

Apply in order; stop at the first that decides. When torn between include and
exclude, answer uncertain and name the condition that cannot be evaluated.

## Include

I1 — The paper ...
I2 — The paper ...

## Exclude

E1 — Not a research paper (editorial, erratum, call for papers).
E2 — Review or survey without a contribution of its own.
E3 — Outside the review's domain.

## Uncertain

U1 — The text is too short to evaluate a criterion that matters.
